# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

One primary viewer: Niels Velthof, owner of Velthof Milieutechniek (Oldenzaal, NL) — manufacturer of stainless-steel (RVS) waste compactors sold direct from the factory. He views this deck after saying "Verras me", likely on his phone (Android) or a desktop browser, possibly opened from an email link. He has been let down twice by a larger agency (Media Kanjers) that never delivered; he is curious but has no patience for fluff. Secondary viewer: Robert (Studio Orange), who presents the deck live during the intake conversation.

## Product Purpose

A six-slide interactive HTML pitch deck that replaces a PowerPoint. It must (1) prove by its own craft that Studio Orange can deliver the "digitaal platform van wereldklasse" it promises, (2) confront the outdated current site (frozen since Dec 2023, Divi bloat, no lead generation) without insulting it, and (3) convert Niels into scheduling the intake conversation. Success = Niels replies to plan the meeting.

## Brand Personality

Nuchter, vakkundig, direct. Dutch plain-spoken tone: short sentences, concrete claims, zero marketing superlatives. The visual language borrows from the machines themselves — steel, pressure, precision. Motion is controlled and hydraulic, never playful. Matches the concept site already built (dark staal palette, velthof-groen accent, Archivo display / Public Sans / Spline Sans Mono).

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS pitch decks: gradient meshes, glassmorphism, hero-metric stat rows.
- PowerPoint template feel: bullet lists, clip art, stock handshake photos.
- The current velthofmilieutechniek.nl Divi look: dated, default, non-industrial.
- Agency self-congratulation: the deck talks about Niels's business, not about Studio Orange.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach** — the deck itself is the proof of quality; every interaction must feel engineered, not decorated.
2. **Machine-grade motion** — animations move like hydraulics: decisive, damped, purposeful. Nothing bounces.
3. **Confront with respect** — name the problems concretely (2023 freeze, no leads) while crediting what worked.
4. **Two clicks to the point** — every slide has one message; the deck ends in one clear action (plan the intake).
5. **Same DNA as the concept site** — reuse its tokens, fonts, and voice so deck and site read as one system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation (arrow keys) and visible focus states required. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling slide/reveal animation. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast on dark surfaces. Touch targets ≥ 44px on mobile; deck must work fully on Android phones.
